# Nipange — Product Vision

> Long-term product direction, agreed with the client. Use this document as the
> reference when making product and design decisions. Not every capability
> described here exists in the current application — do not present unbuilt
> functionality as operational (in UI copy, docs, or marketing).

## What is Nipange?

Nipange is a local discovery and community connection platform that brings
people, businesses and event organizers together.

Its purpose is to help people discover what's happening around them, find
interesting people, discover local businesses and participate in activities
within their communities.

The platform works in both directions: people discover businesses and events,
while businesses and organizers discover and connect with people around them.

## The Three Main Ecosystems

### 1. People

Ordinary users should be able to:

- Discover people around their area.
- Find other people with shared interests.
- Connect and communicate with other users through messaging.
- Discover local activities and events.
- Join events organized by other people.
- Create their own events — Saturday hangouts, football meetups, game nights,
  networking sessions and other social activities.
- Allow people nearby to discover and join their events.

### 2. Businesses

Businesses should be able to:

- Create and manage business listings.
- Make their businesses discoverable to people around their location.
- Showcase their products, services and business information.
- Connect and communicate with potential customers.
- Create awareness about their businesses through the platform.
- Potentially organize or promote events and activities.

### 3. Event Organizers

Event organizers should be able to:

- Create and publish events.
- Reach people around their event location.
- Provide event details, images, dates, locations and participation information.
- Allow interested users to discover and join their events.
- Communicate with interested participants.

## The Bigger Vision

Nipange should become the go-to platform for discovering what's happening
around you and connecting with the people and businesses in your community.

Someone opening Nipange should be able to answer:

- Who is around me?
- What businesses are near me?
- What interesting events are happening this weekend?
- Who shares my interests?
- Is anyone organizing a Saturday hangout?
- Can I organize something and invite people around me?

## Core Product Philosophy

1. **Local discovery** — help users discover relevant people, businesses and
   events around them.
2. **Community** — encourage genuine interactions and community participation.
3. **Accessibility** — make it easy for ordinary people, businesses and event
   organizers to participate.
4. **Connection** — discovery should lead to meaningful communication and
   engagement.
5. **Simplicity** — the platform should be intuitive and easy to use.
6. **Inclusivity** — serve different communities, interests and types of
   businesses.

## Principles for future development

- "Nearby" always means permission-based local discovery, never unrestricted
  real-time tracking. Communicate it that way in copy and UX.
- Prefer features that close the loop: discover → connect → participate.
- Keep the product legible to ordinary users on modest phones and slow
  networks — it is built first for Nairobi and Kenyan communities.
